# Trend Taxonomy

## Trend Type

| Type | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 连板趋势 | At least 2 consecutive limit-up days or limit-up relay structure. |
| 持续上涨 | Multi-day upward slope with staged cumulative gains. |
| 持续下跌 | Multi-day downward slope with staged cumulative losses. |
| 震荡走强 | Range-bound but rising lows/highs, improving MA/capital structure. |

## Stage

| Stage | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 启动 | Early slope or fundamental change starts pricing in. |
| 发酵 | Trend is recognized and capital participation broadens. |
| 高潮 | Crowded trend, fast price expansion, high attention. |
| 尾声 | Root cause weakens, capital exits, or risk dominates. |

## Root Dimension

Use these dimensions only:

- 上游原材料供需
- 行业政策红利
- 公司财报业绩
- 产业链景气度
- 长期资金布局
- 赛道逻辑

## Sustainability Rule

Trend sustainability is decided by root-cause persistence:

- `存在`: trend can extend.
- `减弱`: trend enters critical point and may move into oscillation.
- `消失`: trend may reverse or end.

Pure capital/emotion trends should be marked as weak sustainability.

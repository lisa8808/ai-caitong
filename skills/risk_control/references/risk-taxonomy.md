# Risk Taxonomy

## Risk Level

| Level | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 低 | No clear risk signal; normal fluctuation. |
| 中 | One risk signal active; monitor closely. |
| 高 | Multiple risk signals or one high-confidence negative signal; reduce or exit recommended. |
| 极高 | Catastrophic signal: trend root gone + abnormal drop + capital outflow; stop-loss or take-profit immediately. |

## Risk Source

| Source | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| 资金 | Consecutive main capital outflow, LHB net sell, algorithmic retreat. |
| 公告 | Reduction announcement, earnings shock, regulatory inquiry, compliance penalty. |
| 政策 | Sector regulation tightening, unfavorable industry policy change. |
| 趋势 | Uptrend root weakening, slope flattening, breakdown from consolidation range. |
| 情绪 | Theme retreat, sector-wide drop, batch limit-down in high-position stocks. |

## Operation Rules

When trend root cause is gone, risk is automatically high → recommend reduce or exit.

When abnormal drop plus no supporting capital → risk is spreading → recommend stop-loss.

When positive catalyst exhausted and next-day divergence appears → auto-flag take-profit risk.

Confidence hierarchy for negative signals:
- Official announcement, regulatory inquiry, earnings shock → high confidence.
- Self-media rumors → low confidence.

## Priority

Holdings > watchlist > market-wide. Holdings' risk must be displayed first with full diagnosis.
